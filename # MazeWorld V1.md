# MazeWorld V1.0

## Mechanics

### Player classes
desc: GenAI LLM generated classes based on the environment. 
Each game should have a:
- warrior class, something like a fighter or barbarian
- magic glass, something like a mage or wizard
- healer, something like a cleric or paladin
- joker, something like a rogue or trickster

Warrior wields a weapon, maybe a shield but has no magic. They do have utility abilities that can be used for quests. 
Mage wields a simple weapon, like a staff, and has magic with utility spells, damage spells. 
Healer wields a simple weapon, like a staff, and has magic with utility spells, healing spells, buff spells.
Joker/Jestur has random stats and the gamble ability which does a random effect every turn if they use it. They also can get a random smathering of abilities like spells, etc

Note: utility spells/abilities should be useful for quests.

When I say randomly generated by environment, I mean: 
in a city, the warrior may be a knight or mercenary whereas in the forest they may be a ranger or woodcutter. 
They can also be historical types like viking or samurai.

### Combat
#### Melee
- give the player a weapon. The weapon has a stat, damage type, and dice.
- combat is turn based and starts with whoever has the best ac roll (1d20 + dex)
- combat goes character (player or npc) rolls to hit (1d20 + modifier (based on weapon stat) + level modifier) vs the target DC (10 + armor + dex) 
- if miss, next turn. if hit, character rolls damage (1dX + modifier (based on weapon stat))

#### magic
Magic should be among these five elements

Fire -> Water -> Forest; light -> Dark. 

Each should have a healing spell of various potency, a buff skill, and damage spells. 

Fire beats forest, Forest beats Water, Water beats fire. Light and dark beat each other. 

For now, mage/healer characters can select one magic type and should be able to select 2 spells. 

Mage/Healer spells should be different. 

A jester/joker can not select but may be randomly assigned a spell or ability.

We want combat to be useful, dice based, and enjoyable - not a grind.
